FEATURED CASE STUDY AI Proctoring · Multimodal Computer Vision

ExamGuard AI

An automated examination platform engineered around multimodal proctoring, evidence collection, and human-in-the-loop review.

SYSTEM PHILOSOPHY

AI models are statistical signal detectors, not judges. ExamGuard captures timestamped behavioral anomalies for human proctors to audit, eliminating false-positive disqualifications.

Signal Telemetry & Gaze Tracker Prototype ACTIVE EXPERIMENT

01 The Problem

Remote proctoring tools either suffer from human proctor fatigue at scale, or deploy black-box automated bans that wrongly punish students for natural eye movements and lighting changes.

02 The Approach

Designed a non-intrusive vision pipeline that evaluates webcam frames for specific anomalies (head orientation, gaze deflection, person count) and bundles frame captures with audit logs.

03 Architecture

Python & OpenCV edge detection feeds metadata to a central verification queue. Human reviewers inspect flagged timestamps with side-by-side evidence before taking any administrative action.

04 Engineering Focus & Status

Minimizing edge latency while maintaining robustness against variable webcam angles and low illumination. Currently benchmarking detector thresholds across heterogeneous webcams.
