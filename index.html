FEATURED PROJECT AI Proctoring / Multimodal AI

ExamGuard AI

An automated examination platform focused on multimodal proctoring, evidence collection, human review, and scalable exam management.

01 The Problem

Remote assessments struggle with integrity risks, while rigid automated systems frequently produce high false positives and lack explainability. Educational institutions need defensible evidence rather than black-box automated bans.

02 What I Built

Designed an exam supervision framework that combines video and audio telemetry to flag suspicious behavioral cues (gaze deflection, absent student, multiple faces) while leaving final verdicts strictly to human reviewers.

03 How It Works

Webcam video feeds are processed through lightweight computer vision detectors. When anomaly thresholds are crossed, timestamped video frames and audit metadata are logged into a structured incident queue for human-in-the-loop inspection.

04 My Role & Status

Architected the end-to-end pipeline, vision signal thresholds, and evidence packaging mechanism. Currently benchmarking signal reliability across diverse lighting conditions.
