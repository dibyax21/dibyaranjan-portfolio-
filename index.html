FEATURED CASE STUDY Multimodal AI · Computer Vision Active Prototype

ExamGuard AI

Multimodal exam proctoring framework engineered for evidence collection and human-in-the-loop review.

CORE PHILOSOPHY

AI models are statistical signal detectors, not judges. ExamGuard captures timestamped behavioral anomalies for human proctors to audit, eliminating false-positive automated bans.

01 The Problem

Remote proctoring tools either suffer from human fatigue at scale or rely on rigid algorithms that penalize students for natural head turns or lighting shifts.

02 What I Built

A non-intrusive edge vision pipeline that evaluates webcam streams for specific behavioral anomalies (gaze drift, multiple faces, absent student) and logs timestamped evidence packets.

03 How It Works

OpenCV processes camera frames in real time. Flagged anomalies generate snapshot captures with telemetry metadata for proctors to inspect side-by-side before taking action.

04 Status & Hard Problems

Minimizing edge inference latency and tuning threshold heuristics across variable webcam angles and low-light environments.
